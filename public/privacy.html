Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The Compound Wellness ("we," "us") provides in-home wellness and concierge health services in Orange County, California. This policy explains what personal information we collect through compoundoc.com, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you book, inquire, RSVP to an event, or contact us: name, email, phone number, city, and home address for in-home sessions.
  • Booking details: the practitioner, service, date and time you request.
  • Notes you choose to share, such as what you are hoping to address or special requests. Please do not include sensitive medical details in website forms; your practitioner will discuss those with you directly.
  • Payment information is entered with our payment processor, Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Website usage data (pages viewed, general location, device type) collected through Google Analytics.
  • Member portal accounts are handled by our sign-in provider, Clerk.

How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries, schedule and confirm sessions, and send reminders and confirmations.
  • To process payments after your practitioner confirms a session.
  • To send you updates and offers if you have joined our email list. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed for them to do so:

  • Airtable — stores inquiries and booking requests.
  • Klaviyo — manages our email list and marketing emails.
  • Google (Gmail, Calendar, Analytics) — sends our emails, schedules sessions, and measures website traffic.
  • Stripe — processes payments.
  • Clerk — member portal sign-in.
  • Slack — internal alerts to our team when an inquiry or RSVP arrives (name, practitioner and timing only).
  • Railway — hosts this website.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect safety.

Health information. Information you submit through this website's forms is not a substitute for a clinical record, and the website is not intended for urgent medical needs. If you have a medical emergency, call 911. Clinical records created in the course of your care are handled separately by your practitioner under applicable health-privacy laws.

How we protect it

The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), access to our administrative tools requires a password, and we limit who can see inquiries and bookings. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics, which uses cookies and similar technologies to measure website traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings or use Google's opt-out tools.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you marketing email. California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. To make a request, email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
